Acai bowls in Rogers, Bentonville and the rest of NWA

Playa Bowls Rogers is the first Playa Bowls shop in Arkansas. It sits in Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center on S 46th Street in the Pinnacle Hills area of Rogers, a short drive from Bentonville, Lowell and Cave Springs.

The menu is built on six blended fruit bases: acai, pitaya, coconut, mango, green and banana. Every base is dairy free. Bowls come topped with Playa's blueberry flax granola, fresh fruit and a drizzle. Smoothies are blended from real fruit and plant-based milk. Catering packages feed 10 to 100 people.

Hours will be 8 am to 8 pm every day of the week. Find hours, parking and directions, read the menu, or book catering for your office, school or team.

About Playa Bowls

Playa Bowls began in 2014 as a sidewalk cart in Belmar, New Jersey, run by two surfers, Rob Giuliani and Abby Taylor. The cart became a shop, and the shop became a national brand with more than 300 locations. The acai, pitaya and coconut used in the bases are organic.

The Rogers shop is owned and operated by Acumen Franchise Group, a Northwest Arkansas company. Further Northwest Arkansas locations will follow.

Follow the shop on Instagram at @playabowlsrogers_nwa for opening-week news and seasonal specials.
