Smoothies

Smoothies in Rogers, AR

Real fruit blended with coconut or cashew milk, in 16 and 20 oz cups. No syrups, no dairy base. Add protein to any smoothie. Open 8 am to 8 pm at Summit at Pinnacle Springs.

What goes in a Playa smoothie

Fruit, and a plant-based milk to blend it. The Strawberry Banana is strawberry, banana and coconut milk. The Aloha is banana, mango, pineapple and coconut milk. The Dolce is banana, chocolate protein, peanut butter and coconut milk, built for after the gym. The Pink Pitaya puts dragon fruit in a cup.

Any smoothie can take a scoop of vanilla or chocolate whey protein. Whey is the one add-on with dairy in it; everything else in the fruit smoothies is plant based.

Surf Quenchers

Surf Quenchers are Playa's lighter fruit drinks for days when a full smoothie is too much. Flavors rotate with the season. Ask what is on the board.

Smoothie or bowl?

Same fruit, different form. A bowl is blended thick and eaten with a spoon, with granola and fruit on top. A smoothie is blended thinner and goes in a cup for the drive to Bentonville. If you have never had acai, start with the bowl guide.

A smoothie shop near Pinnacle Hills

Playa Bowls Rogers is at 1803 S 46th Street, Suite 134, in Summit at Pinnacle Springs with Whole Foods, just west of I-49. Open 8 am to 8 pm, seven days a week, from Saturday, October 24, 2026. Directions and parking.

Ordering for a group? Individual smoothies can be packed with names on the lids through catering.

Smoothie questions

What sizes do smoothies come in?

16 and 20 ounces.

Are Playa Bowls smoothies dairy free?

The fruit smoothies are blended with coconut or cashew milk, not dairy milk. Whey protein is the one add-on that contains dairy.

Can I add protein?

Yes. Vanilla or chocolate whey protein can go in any smoothie or bowl.

Which smoothies are vegan?

Strawberry Banana and Aloha are vegan as listed. Ask the team about the rest; most are a honey-free swap away.
