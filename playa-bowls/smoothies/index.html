Smoothies

Smoothies in Rogers, AR

Real fruit blended with coconut or cashew milk, in 16 and 20 oz cups. Add protein to any smoothie. Coming soon to Summit at Pinnacle Springs.

What goes in a Playa smoothie

Fruit, and a plant-based milk to blend it. The Strawberry Banana is strawberry, banana and coconut milk. The Aloha is banana, mango, pineapple and coconut milk. The Dolce is banana, chocolate protein, peanut butter and coconut milk, built for after the gym. The Pink Pitaya puts dragon fruit in a cup.

Any smoothie can take a scoop of vanilla or chocolate whey protein.

Surf Quenchers

Surf Quenchers are Playa's shaken refreshers: a superfruit base with fruit puree, organic agave and lemon juice, poured over ice and made to order. The lineup is Mango Break (mango base, mango puree), Watermelon Swell (pitaya base, watermelon and strawberry purees) and Acai Splash (acai base, strawberry puree), with an optional plant-based energy boost of about 70 mg of caffeine from green coffee bean extract.

Smoothie or bowl?

Same fruit, different form. A bowl is blended thick and eaten with a spoon, with granola and fruit on top. A smoothie is blended thinner and goes in a cup for the drive to Bentonville. If you have never had acai, start with the bowl guide.

A smoothie shop near Pinnacle Hills

Playa Bowls Rogers is at 1803 S 46th Street, Suite 134, in Summit at Pinnacle Springs with Whole Foods, east of I-49 at the New Hope Road exit. Coming soon; hours will be 8 am to 8 pm, seven days a week. Directions and parking.

Smoothie questions

What sizes do smoothies come in?

16 and 20 ounces.

Can I add protein?

Yes. Vanilla or chocolate whey protein can go in any smoothie or bowl.
