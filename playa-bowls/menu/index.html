Menu

Playa Bowls Rogers menu

Bowls built on six blended fruit bases, smoothies made with real fruit and plant-based milk, Surf Quenchers and protein add-ons. Bowls are one size. Prices are posted in the shop and in the Playa Bowls app.

How a Playa bowl works

Pick a base, pick your toppings, pick a drizzle. Every base is a fruit base, blended thick, served in a bowl and finished with Playa's blueberry flax granola. The signature bowls below are the shortcuts. Build your own if you want something different.

For ingredient, nutrition and allergen information, see playabowls.com or ask the team in the shop before you order.

The bases

Playa Acai

Organic acai from the rainforests of Brazil, imported straight into New Jersey. Not blended with any juices. The original.

Playa Pitaya

Organic pitaya, the pink dragon fruit, grown in Nicaragua. Non-GMO.

Playa Coconut

The raw insides of young Thai coconuts, organic, blended with banana.

Mango

Made from Alphonso mangos grown in India.

Green

Playa's kale-based green base.

Banana

Surf Quenchers and protein

Surf Quenchers

Playa's shaken refreshers: a superfruit base with fruit puree, organic agave and lemon juice, poured over ice and made to order. Flavors include Mango Break, Watermelon Swell and Acai Splash. Add a plant-based energy boost of about 70 mg of caffeine from green coffee bean extract.

Protein add-onProtein packed

Add vanilla or chocolate whey protein to any bowl or smoothie.

Protein bites
