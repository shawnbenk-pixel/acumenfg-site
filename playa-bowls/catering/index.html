Catering that people actually eat

A Playa Bowls bowl bar is a different kind of office breakfast. We bring the bases, the fruit, the drizzles and the granola. Your group builds their own bowls. It works for Monday meetings in Bentonville, teacher appreciation week in Rogers, post-game team meals, church groups, and birthday parties.

Catering starts the day the shop opens, and you can book a date now. Everything is made the morning of your event at the Rogers shop and packed cold. Catering is priced from the same menu as the shop, so there is no markup for ordering in bulk.

To book, email info@acumenfg.com with the date, the headcount and pickup or delivery. We reply with a quote and a menu to choose from.

Packages

Build-your-own bowl bar

Sized from 10 to 100 people. A 10-person package has one base, three fresh fruits, two drizzles and granola. A 100-person package has ten bases, three fruits and six drizzles.

Bases: acai, pitaya, coconut, mango, green, banana.
Fruit: blueberry, pineapple, banana, strawberry, mango, kiwi.
Drizzles: peanut butter, honey, almond butter, Nutella.

Fruit trays

One tray serves 15. Kiwi, blueberry, pineapple and strawberry with your choice of base for dipping.

Individual bowls and smoothies

Any bowl or smoothie from the menu, made to Playa standards and packed individually with names on the lids. Easiest option for mixed groups and for lunch-and-learns.

Half-gallon base tubs are also available for larger groups that want to set up their own bar.

Catering questions

How much notice do you need for catering?

Please order at least 48 hours ahead for standard packages, and a week ahead for 50 people or more. Same-week requests are welcome; we will tell you quickly whether we can fill them.

Do you deliver catering?

Yes, in and around Rogers and Bentonville, including Lowell, Cave Springs, Centerton and Bella Vista. Pickup at the shop is always available. Tell us which you prefer when you order.

How much does Playa Bowls catering cost?

Catering is priced from the same menu as the shop. Email us with your headcount and we will send a quote the same day where possible.

What comes in a build-your-own bowl package?

A 10-person package includes one base, three fresh fruits, two drizzles and Playa's blueberry flax granola. Packages scale up to 100 people with ten bases and six drizzles. You choose the bases, fruits and drizzles.

Can you handle dairy-free, vegan or nut-free groups?

Every base is dairy free. For a vegan setup we skip honey and whey protein. For nut-sensitive groups we leave out peanut butter and almond butter, but the shop and the granola facility handle nuts, so we cannot guarantee a nut-free order.

Do you do birthday parties?

Yes. Build-your-own bowl bars work well for birthday parties, team parties and school events. Ask about the party package when you book.
