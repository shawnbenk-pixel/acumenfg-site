        Your order

          How team orders work

          • Order by 6 pm the day before. Orders of 25 items or more need 48 hours.
          • $50 minimum.
          • Every item is made to order at the Rogers shop and labeled with the name you give it, so 40 different bowls arrive sorted by athlete.
          • Adds and extras are $1.00 each. Each ingredient you take off a bowl covers one, so a swap costs nothing.
          • Bowls are packed cold with ice packs and timed to your ready-by time. Smoothies are best served on arrival.
          • Changes are fine up to 6 hours before. Cancellations inside 24 hours carry a 25% fee.
          • We confirm your order and send a Clover invoice by email. Pay by card from the invoice.

          About the macros

          Macros are Playa's published values for each item at its standard build, from the nutrition tool at playabowls.com/nutrition. Adds, extras and anything you take off adjust them using the same source. Playa's values come from lab testing, published resources and supplier data, so expect some variation in what you receive. Protein items use Playa Power protein.

          For ingredient and allergen information, see playabowls.com or ask the team in the shop before you order.
