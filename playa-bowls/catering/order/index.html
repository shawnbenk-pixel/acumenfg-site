      Your order

        How team orders work

        • Order by 6 pm Central the day before your event. Orders of 25 items or more need 48 hours before the ready-by time.
        • The minimum order is $50.
        • We make every item to order at our Rogers shop. Put a name on any item and we'll label it for that person.
        • Add-ons and extra toppings are $1.00 each. Swaps are free. If you take a topping off a bowl, you can add a different one at no charge.
        • Bowls are packed cold with ice packs and ready at the time you choose.
        • You can change your order up to 6 hours ahead. Cancelling within 24 hours of your event has a 25% fee.
        • After you send your order, we'll email you a confirmation and an invoice. Pay the invoice online by card.

        About the macros

        The macros come from Playa Bowls' published nutrition information at playabowls.com/nutrition, and they are for each item as it is normally made. If you add or remove a topping, we adjust the numbers using Playa's figures for that topping. Playa's numbers are based on lab testing, published resources and supplier data, so the item you receive may vary a little. Our high-protein items are made with Playa Power protein.

        For ingredient and allergen information, visit playabowls.com or ask our team at the shop before you order.
