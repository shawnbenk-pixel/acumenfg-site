What is an acai bowl?

An acai bowl is frozen acai fruit blended thick, served in a bowl, and topped with granola, fresh fruit and a drizzle. Here is what acai is, what it tastes like, and how Playa Bowls Rogers builds one.

The fruit

Acai (ah-sah-EE) is the small, dark purple fruit of a palm that grows in the Amazon basin. Most of the world's acai comes from Brazil. The fruit does not travel fresh, so it is pulped and frozen within a day of harvest. That frozen pulp is what every acai bowl starts with.

On its own, acai tastes like an earthy berry with a note of dark chocolate. It is not very sweet. That is why acai bowls are topped with fruit, granola and something sweet like honey.

The bowl

A bowl is a thick blend of frozen acai pulp and fruit, served cold with a spoon. The texture sits between sorbet and a very thick smoothie. Playa Bowls blends its organic acai with banana for body, then finishes the bowl with blueberry flax granola, fresh fruit and a drizzle of honey, peanut butter, almond butter or Nutella.

Bowls are one size. A plain bowl like the 8th Ave (acai, granola, banana, honey) is the lightest. The Power Bowl adds chocolate protein and peanut butter for a post-workout meal. The full lineup is on the menu page.

Acai is one of six bases

Pitaya

Pink dragon fruit. Milder and sweeter than acai, bright pink in the bowl. The Electric Mermaid and Dragonberry are built on it.

Coconut

Organic coconut blended with banana. Creamy, light flavor. The Nutelloco is the signature coconut bowl.

Mango, green and banana

Mango for tropical sweetness, green for a fruit-and-greens blend, banana for the mellowest base of the six.

Common questions

How do you pronounce acai?

Ah-sah-EE. It is a Portuguese word; the fruit comes from the Amazon basin in Brazil.

Is an acai bowl healthy?

The base is fruit blended thick, with no dairy. What you add decides the rest. Fresh fruit and granola keep it light. Honey, nut butter and Nutella add sugar and calories. A plain bowl like the 8th Ave is the lightest option on the menu.

Does an acai bowl have dairy?

Not at Playa Bowls. Every base is dairy free. Nutella, white chocolate and whey protein are the toppings that contain dairy.

Is an acai bowl vegan?

The bases are. Skip the honey and choose plant-based toppings and the bowl is vegan.

What does acai taste like?

Earthy berry with a hint of dark chocolate, less sweet than most fruit. That is why acai bowls usually get fruit and a drizzle on top.

What is the difference between acai and pitaya?

Acai is a dark purple palm fruit with an earthy flavor. Pitaya is pink dragon fruit, milder and sweeter. Playa Bowls serves both as bases, plus coconut, mango, green and banana.
