The shop

Where is Playa Bowls in Rogers, Arkansas?

1803 S 46th Street, Suite 134, Rogers, AR 72758, inside Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center on S 46th Street in the Pinnacle Hills area, just west of I-49. Free parking in the center's lot.

What are Playa Bowls Rogers hours?

8 am to 8 pm, seven days a week once the shop opens. The Google listing will carry today's hours.

When is the grand opening?

Coming soon. The date will be announced on Instagram and on this site first.

Is Playa Bowls Rogers locally owned?

Yes. The shop is independently owned and operated by Acumen Franchise Group, a Northwest Arkansas company, under a franchise agreement with Playa Bowls.

Is this the first Playa Bowls in Arkansas?

Yes. Rogers is the first Playa Bowls shop in Arkansas. Further Northwest Arkansas locations will follow.

Food and allergens

What is an acai bowl?

Frozen acai fruit blended thick, served in a bowl and topped with granola, fresh fruit and a drizzle. See the acai bowl guide.

Are the bowls dairy free?

Every base is dairy free. The toppings that contain dairy are Nutella, white chocolate and whey protein.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. Skip the honey and choose plant-based toppings. The Strawberry Banana and Aloha smoothies are vegan as listed.

Do you have nut-free options?

Several bowls are built without nut ingredients, but the shop works with peanuts and tree nuts every day and the granola is made in a facility that handles nuts. We cannot guarantee a nut-free product.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Gluten-free granola is available on request, with a slight risk of cross-contact.

What allergens are in the shop?

Playa Bowls shops handle peanuts, tree nuts, soy, milk, eggs and wheat, and cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. Tell the team about a severe allergy before you order.

Are the bowls one size?

Yes. One size, built to be a meal. Kids often share a bowl or order a 16 oz smoothie.

Ordering

Can I order ahead?

Once the shop is open, order ahead in the Playa Bowls app, or walk in any time from 8 am to 8 pm.

Do you deliver?

Third-party delivery will be announced on this site and on Instagram when it goes live. Catering delivery is available now by email.

Do you have a rewards program?

Yes. Playa Rewards runs in the Playa Bowls app and earns points on every order.

Do you sell gift cards?

Playa Bowls gift cards will be sold in the shop.

Do you cater?

Yes. Build-your-own bowl bars for 10 to 100 people, fruit trays, and individual bowls and smoothies, for pickup or delivery across NWA. See catering.
