Where to find us

The shop is in Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center on S 46th Street in the Pinnacle Hills area of Rogers, east of I-49 at the New Hope Road exit. Parking is in the center's lot. The Vista at Summit apartments sit next door.

Open in Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions from Bentonville, Lowell, Springdale or anywhere in Northwest Arkansas.

Hours

Hours will be 8 am to 8 pm, seven days a week. Once the doors open, the Google listing carries today's hours.

Opening

Coming soon. Rogers is the first Playa Bowls in Arkansas. The opening date will be announced first on @playabowlsrogers_nwa.

What we serve

Acai and superfruit bowls

Six bases: acai, pitaya, coconut, mango, green and banana. Topped with Playa's blueberry flax granola, fresh fruit, and honey or nut butter. What is an acai bowl?

Smoothies

Real fruit blended with coconut or cashew milk, in 16 and 20 oz sizes. Add protein to any smoothie. Smoothies in Rogers

Catering

Build-your-own bowl bars for 10 to 100 people, fruit trays and individual bowls for offices, schools and teams across NWA. Catering details

For ingredient, nutrition and allergen information, see playabowls.com or ask the team in the shop before you order. Full lineup on the menu page.

Ordering, rewards and delivery

Once the doors open, walk in any time from 8 am to 8 pm, or order ahead in the Playa Bowls app and earn Playa Rewards points on every visit. Third-party delivery will be announced here when it goes live.

Catering orders for pickup or local delivery go to info@acumenfg.com. Give us the date, the headcount and whether you want pickup or delivery.
