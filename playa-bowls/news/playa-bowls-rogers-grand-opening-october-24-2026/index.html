Playa Bowls opens its first Arkansas shop in Rogers on October 24

Playa Bowls Rogers opens Saturday, October 24, 2026 at Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center on S 46th Street. It is the first Playa Bowls in Arkansas.

Rogers, Arkansas, October 10, 2026. Playa Bowls, the acai bowl brand that started as a sidewalk cart in Belmar, New Jersey, opens its first Arkansas shop on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Playa Bowls Rogers is at 1803 S 46th Street, Suite 134, Rogers, AR 72758, in Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center in the Pinnacle Hills area just west of I-49.

What to expect

The shop serves bowls built on six dairy-free fruit bases (acai, pitaya, coconut, mango, green and banana), real-fruit smoothies in 16 and 20 oz sizes, Surf Quenchers and protein bites. Bowls are one size and come topped with Playa's blueberry flax granola, fresh fruit and a drizzle. The full menu is posted on this site.

Hours are 8 am to 8 pm, seven days a week. Parking is free in the center's lot.

Catering from day one

Build-your-own bowl bars for 10 to 100 people, fruit trays and individually packed bowls and smoothies are available for pickup or delivery across Rogers and Bentonville. Requests go to info@acumenfg.com. Catering details.

Locally owned

Playa Bowls Rogers is owned and operated by Acumen Franchise Group, a Northwest Arkansas company. The Rogers shop is the first of several planned for Northwest Arkansas.

Opening week

Opening-week details, including any specials, will be posted on Instagram at @playabowlsrogers_nwa and on the Rogers shop page.

The shop is hiring. Write to recruiting@acumenfg.com.
