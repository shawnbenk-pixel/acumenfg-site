Playa Bowls is coming to Rogers, Arkansas

Playa Bowls Rogers is coming soon to Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center on S 46th Street. It is the first Playa Bowls in Arkansas.

Rogers, Arkansas, October 10, 2026. Playa Bowls, the acai bowl brand that started as a sidewalk cart in Belmar, New Jersey, is bringing its first Arkansas shop to Rogers. Playa Bowls Rogers is coming soon to 1803 S 46th Street, Suite 134, Rogers, AR 72758, in Summit at Pinnacle Springs, the Whole Foods-anchored center in the Pinnacle Hills area just west of I-49.

What to expect

The shop serves bowls built on six dairy-free fruit bases (acai, pitaya, coconut, mango, green and banana), real-fruit smoothies in 16 and 20 oz sizes, Surf Quenchers and protein bites. Bowls are one size and come topped with Playa's blueberry flax granola, fresh fruit and a drizzle. The full menu is posted on this site.

Hours will be 8 am to 8 pm, seven days a week. Parking is free in the center's lot.

Catering from day one

Build-your-own bowl bars for 10 to 100 people, fruit trays and individually packed bowls and smoothies are available for pickup or delivery across Rogers and Bentonville. Requests go to info@acumenfg.com. Catering details.

Locally owned

Playa Bowls Rogers is owned and operated by Acumen Franchise Group, a Northwest Arkansas company. The Rogers shop is the first of several planned for Northwest Arkansas.

Opening date

The opening date, and any opening specials, will be posted first on Instagram at @playabowlsrogers_nwa and on the Rogers shop page.

The shop is hiring. Write to recruiting@acumenfg.com.
