The Firm

Acumen is an operating company, not a holding company. Its owners select the sites and negotiate the leases, and an owner‑operator runs the shops day to day. Every location is opened on the same discipline: a proven brand in strong real estate, run hands‑on.

Brands

Acumen is built to operate multiple franchise brands.

Playa Bowls

First platform · Northwest Arkansas

A national fast‑casual brand built around acai and superfruit bowls, smoothies and juice. Small footprints and simple operations make it well suited to multi‑unit ownership. Acumen holds a multi‑unit development agreement with Playa Bowls and is bringing the brand to Northwest Arkansas.

Locations

The first shop is being developed at the Summit at Pinnacle Springs, a Whole Foods‑anchored center in Rogers. Further Northwest Arkansas locations will follow.
